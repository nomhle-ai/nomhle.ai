The Technology

Advanced Wastewater Solution

The Bio-Separator harnesses natural biological processes to break down waste on-site — no chemicals, no honey-sucking, no contamination of the land or water around it.

Natural Cleaning

Naturally occurring bacteria and enzymes digest waste, leaving clean, treated water behind.

Water Safety

Pathogens are broken down through the treatment cycle, protecting families and communities.

Groundwater Protection

A sealed separation chamber prevents seepage, keeping the surrounding soil and aquifers clean.

Enzyme Technology

Exo-enzymes accelerate decomposition, making the system fast, reliable and self-sustaining.

The Process

Simple 4-Step Process

From waste to clean water through a continuous natural cycle.

01

Waste Enters

Waste from the flush toilet flows into the sealed Bio-Separator chamber below ground.

02

Exo-Enzymes

Exo-enzymes are released to begin breaking the solid waste down into simpler matter.

03

Bacteria Digest

Natural bacteria digest the broken-down waste, consuming pathogens in the process.

04

Clean Water

Treated, clean water disperses safely into the soak-away, leaving no harmful residue.

Installation

Quick & Easy Installation

A proven six-step installation process, carried out by our trained teams across communities and municipalities.

1

Site Identification

The optimal location is assessed and marked for the unit and its soak-away.

2

Excavation

The hole is dug to the correct depth and dimensions for the Bio-Separator.

3

Placement & Backfill

The unit is set in place, levelled and backfilled to secure it firmly.

4

Soak-Away & Pipework

The soak-away is built and all pipework is connected to the chamber.

5

Top Structure

The toilet top structure is erected over the unit, ready for use.

6

Water Supply & Anchor

Water supply is connected and the structure anchored — ready to flush.
